Will Do · Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Will Do does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The app has no server; everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

Data stored on your device

  • Reminders, shopping items, places and photos you add are stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. They are included in your regular iPhone or iCloud device backup, the same as other apps.
  • Backups you export from Settings are files you save yourself (for example to Files or iCloud Drive). The app does not upload them anywhere.

Permissions

  • Camera and Photos: only to attach pictures to a reminder or shopping item, when you choose to.
  • Location (while using the app): to search for stores on the map and to notify you when you arrive at a place you turned alerts on for. Arrival is detected by iOS on your device; your location is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • Notifications: to deliver reminders you set up.
  • Siri: when you add a reminder with Siri, speech is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The app only receives the resulting text.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs. Map search uses Apple Maps. If you tap an address, it opens in Google Maps (app or website) at your request, and Google's privacy policy applies there.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.

不着急 · 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日

不着急不收集任何数据。没有账号，没有统计分析，没有广告，也不追踪你。App 没有服务器，你输入的一切都只留在你的 iPhone 上。

存在设备上的数据

  • 提醒、待买物品、地点和图片只存在 App 在你 iPhone 上的存储里，会和其他 App 一样随 iPhone 或 iCloud 设备备份一起备份。
  • 在设置里导出的备份文件由你自己保存（比如存到"文件"或 iCloud Drive），App 不会上传到任何地方。

权限

  • 相机和照片：只在你选择时，用于给提醒或待买物品附加图片。
  • 定位（使用 App 期间）：用于在地图上搜索商店，并在你到达开启了提醒的地点时通知你。是否到达由 iOS 在设备上判断，你的位置不会发给我们或任何人。
  • 通知：用于发送你设置的提醒。
  • Siri：用 Siri 添加提醒时，语音由 Apple 按照 Apple 隐私政策 处理，App 只收到识别出的文字。

第三方

App 不包含任何第三方 SDK。地图搜索使用 Apple 地图。点击地址时，会按你的操作在 Google 地图（App 或网页）中打开，届时适用 Google 的隐私政策。

儿童

App 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

变更和联系

本政策如有变更会在此页面更新。如有问题，请在 GitHub 上提 issue。
