Will Do · Support

Will Do is a simple reminder and shopping list app for iPhone. Reminders are listed one by one instead of on a calendar, shopping items can remind you when you arrive at a store, and you can add reminders with Siri (Chinese only for now).

Contact

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

Is my data uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything stays on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
How do I move my data to a new phone?
Settings → Export Backup, save the file to Files or iCloud Drive, then use Restore from Backup on the new phone. Your data is also included in normal iPhone backups.
Arrival alerts don't fire.
Allow location access "While Using the App" and allow notifications. Alerts only fire for places with alerts turned on and with items still to buy there. iOS allows up to 20 alert locations.
Siri doesn't recognize the phrase.
Open the app once after installing, set Siri's language to Chinese (Mandarin), and say for example "用不着急添加提醒".

不着急 · 支持

不着急是一个简单的 iPhone 提醒和购物清单 App：提醒逐条列出而不是按日历组织，待买物品可以在你到店时提醒，还可以用 Siri 添加提醒。

联系

遇到问题或有建议，请在 GitHub 上提 issue，并写上 iPhone 型号和 iOS 版本。

常见问题

数据会上传吗？
不会，所有数据都在你的 iPhone 上。详见隐私政策。
换手机怎么迁移数据？
设置 → 导出备份，把文件存到"文件"或 iCloud Drive，在新手机上用"从备份恢复"。数据也会随 iPhone 备份一起备份。
到店提醒不响？
请允许"使用 App 期间"定位和通知。只有开启了提醒、并且还有要在那里买的物品的地点才会提醒。iOS 最多支持 20 个提醒位置。
Siri 认不出来？
装好后先打开一次 App，把 Siri 语言设为中文（普通话），然后说"用不着急添加提醒"。
