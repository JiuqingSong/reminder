## English (U.S.)

**Name**: Will Do

备选（如果 Will Do 被占用）：`Will Do: Reminders & Lists`、`Will Do – Reminder & Shopping`

**Subtitle**: Reminders & store alerts

**Promotional Text**:

Jot it down, stop worrying. Reminders listed one by one, a shopping list that speaks up when you reach the store, and everything stays on your iPhone.

**Description**:

Will Do is a calm, simple reminder app. Instead of a calendar, your reminders are listed one by one, so you always see what's coming up next.

REMINDERS THAT FIT HOW YOU THINK
• Set a date, or a date and time, or no date at all for things you keep in mind all year
• Start reminding days in advance with one daily summary at the time you choose
• Overdue items keep reminding you every day until they're done
• Repeat daily, weekly, monthly or yearly, with custom intervals
• Swipe left to complete; long-press to postpone a day or a week

A SHOPPING LIST THAT KNOWS WHERE YOU ARE
• Add items with text, photos and links, or paste a whole list at once
• Pick where each item can be bought: search stores on the map, add several branches
• Turn on arrival alerts and get a notification when you reach a store that has something on your list
• Recurring items switch between "need to buy" and "check later" instead of disappearing

THE DETAILS
• Attach photos from the camera or your library
• Links, phone numbers and addresses are tappable; addresses open in Google Maps
• Notifications show up on Apple Watch automatically
• English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese
• Add reminders with Siri in Chinese (Mandarin), with dates and times understood from what you say

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything is stored on your iPhone. Export a backup file whenever you like and restore it on another phone.

**Keywords**: reminder,todo,shopping list,grocery,errands,location,arrival,checklist,recurring,notes,alert

## 简体中文

**名称**：不着急

备选：`不着急 - 提醒和购物清单`、`不着急提醒`

**副标题**：说一句就记下，到店提醒买东西

**推广文本**：

记下来，就不用着急了。提醒逐条列出，到了商店会提醒你该买什么，所有数据都只在你的 iPhone 上。

**描述**：

不着急是一个安静、简单的提醒 App。它不按日历组织，而是把提醒一条一条列出来，接下来要做什么一眼就能看到。

按你的习惯提醒
• 可以设日期、精确到分钟，也可以不设日期，作为常年提醒放在顶部
• 提前几天开始提醒，每天在你设定的时间汇总成一条通知
• 过期的事项每天继续提醒，直到完成
• 支持每天、每周、每月、每年重复，可设间隔
• 左划完成，长按可以推迟一天或一周

知道你在哪儿的购物清单
• 物品可以带文字、图片和链接，也可以一次粘贴整张清单
• 给物品选购买地点：在地图上搜索商店，一个地点可以加多家分店
• 开启到店提醒，到了有东西要买的商店就会通知你
• 循环物品在"需要买"和"以后检查"之间切换，不会买完就消失

用 Siri 添加
说"用不着急记一下"，再说"明天下午2点去洗车"，日期和时间自动识别。

细节
• 拍照或从相册选图附加到提醒
• 链接、电话、地址都能直接点开，地址用 Google 地图打开
• 通知会自动转到 Apple Watch
• 支持简体中文、繁体中文和英文

注重隐私
不用注册，没有广告，不追踪。数据全部存在你的 iPhone 上，可以随时导出备份文件，在另一台手机上恢复。

**关键词**：提醒,待办,备忘,购物清单,买菜,到店提醒,定位提醒,清单,日程,重复提醒,Siri

## 审核备注（App Review Information → Notes，英文）

No account or login is required. All data is stored locally on the device; the app has no server.

Arrival alerts: open Shopping (the first row in the list) → top-right places button → add a place by searching the map → turn on "Arrival Alert" for that location. Add an item to the shopping list and assign it that place. When the device enters the 200 m region, a local notification lists the items. Only "While Using the App" location permission is used (UNLocationNotificationTrigger).

Siri: the app provides an App Shortcut to add a reminder. Its phrases are currently Chinese only (e.g. "用不着急添加提醒" with Siri set to Chinese, Mandarin). The same action can be run from the Shortcuts app in any language.

Camera and photo library are only used to attach images to reminders, when the user chooses to.

联系信息：填你的名字、电话、邮箱（只给审核人员看，不公开）。演示账号：不需要，把"需要登录"的勾去掉。
